# Mobile

- Assume narrow screens and mobile keyboards.
- Preserve usable layout when the virtual keyboard opens.
- Avoid desktop-only interactions for critical workflows.
- Test important Studio flows on mobile-sized viewports.
- Do not require hover for essential actions.
- Keep primary build/chat/preview controls reachable on mobile.
